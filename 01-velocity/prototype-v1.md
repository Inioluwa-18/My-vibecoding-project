## The build

- **What I built:** A digital home for spending intentional time with God
- **Tool used:** Replit
- **Shareable link:** https://faith-and-flow.replit.app

## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** A synergy of Spotify and YouVersion
- **What confused them:** The fact that every icon clicked led me to the login page
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** Whether scripture reading and music can happen at the same time
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** I actually intended for a bible app that can have a music streaming function within it, which my partner was able to catch at first glance, but some errors were spotted about each click leading to the login page, and suggestions were made on having a brief of the value the product offers before nudging users to log in.
